HUCKLINE

Privacy Policy

Effective 26 October 2026 · Applies to the Huckline app on Android and iOS

Huckline is a bike stunt game made by Mulhimfy. We built it to collect as little as possible: the game has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no account of its own.

What stays on your device

Your progress (best scores, stars, coins, bikes, paint, rider kit and settings) is saved only on your phone. We never receive it. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data, deletes it.

Google Play Games and Game Center

On Android the game uses Google Play Games Services, and on iOS Apple Game Center, for sign-in, achievements and leaderboards. If you use them, Google or Apple processes your player profile, the achievements you unlock and the scores you submit under their own privacy policies (Google, Apple). These services may also collect diagnostics about how the service works. Huckline does not receive your email address or any contact details. You can use the game without signing in.

What we do not do

  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or crash reporting SDKs.
  • No sale or sharing of personal data.
  • No location, camera, microphone, contacts or photos access.

Deleting your data

Game progress: uninstall Huckline or clear its storage. Google Play Games data: delete it from your Google account at myaccount.google.com (Data & privacy) or in the Play Games app settings. Game Center data: manage it in your Apple ID settings on your device. You can also ask us for help at the address below.

Children

Huckline is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
